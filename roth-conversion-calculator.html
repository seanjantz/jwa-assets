Important disclosures

Assumptions and limitations. This calculator is a hypothetical illustration for educational purposes only. It does not represent any specific investment, portfolio, or annuity product, and it is not a product illustration, an offer, or a recommendation. In investment account mode, growth is assumed at a constant annual rate and is hypothetical; actual returns vary from year to year and can be negative, and no advisory fees or fund expenses are deducted. In indexed annuity mode, index credits are assumed at a constant rate and are hypothetical; actual fixed indexed annuity credits vary by year, may be zero in some years, and are subject to caps, spreads, and participation rates. Premium bonuses are typically subject to vesting schedules and surrender charge periods. The free withdrawal is modeled on the basis you select, either a percentage of the start-of-year account value or the greater of that amount and a percentage of original premium; provisions vary by product.

Taxes and Medicare. Bracket calculations use 2026 federal tax brackets and the standard deduction, held constant in all years, including the additional standard deduction for age 65 and older where ages qualify ($2,050 single, $1,650 per married spouse), and exclude state income tax, the temporary senior deduction and its phase-out, and the net investment income tax. Amounts withheld for taxes are distributions, not conversions, and may be subject to a 10% additional tax before age 59½. IRMAA checks use 2026 MAGI thresholds, held constant in all years; the calculator flags or limits threshold crossings but does not estimate surcharge dollar amounts. Medicare premiums in any year are based on MAGI from two years prior. The Other Income field is treated as MAGI and, less the standard deduction, as taxable income; individual circumstances such as tax-exempt interest add-backs and the taxable portion of Social Security will differ. Required minimum distributions are estimated using the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table, beginning at age 73 for those born before 1960 and age 75 for those born in 1960 or later, and are treated as taxable distributions, not conversions.

Do-nothing comparison. The comparison, when enabled, grows the unconverted IRA with no conversions. In indexed annuity mode it grows at the do-nothing rate you enter, with no premium bonus; in investment account mode both paths use the same assumed annual return. RMDs are taxed at your withholding rate, and the after-tax proceeds are reinvested in a taxable account whose growth is reduced 15% for annual tax drag and which is assumed to pass to heirs with a stepped-up basis. All remaining pre-tax IRA dollars are discounted at your heirs' single entered rate under the 10-year inherited IRA rule. After year 10, the conversion path continues to grow at the assumed annual rate. Both paths use flat annual growth, which ignores sequence-of-returns risk in both directions, and the comparison runs to the age you select (85, 90, or 95). A premium bonus, where one is entered, is included on the conversion side, so the comparison reflects the full strategy, not taxes alone.

Roth conversions are taxable events and cannot be undone. Consult a qualified tax professional before acting.
