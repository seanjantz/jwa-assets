The jargon, translated

The industry has a word for everything.
This book has an answer.

Most retirement advice hides behind terms built to keep you nodding along. Here is the same information, said straight.

They say
Sequence of returns risk
Straight talk

A bad year early in retirement does more damage than the same year later. The order your returns happen in can make or break the plan.

They say
Risk capacity
Straight talk

How big a market drop your finances can actually survive, which is not the same as how big a drop you can stomach emotionally.

They say
IRMAA surcharge
Straight talk

Cross one hidden income line by a single dollar and your Medicare premiums jump for the whole year.

They say
Tax-efficient withdrawal sequencing
Straight talk

The order you pull from your accounts can hand you years of lower taxes, or quietly cost you them.

About the author

Sean Jantz

Sean is the founder of Jantz Wealth Advisors, a retirement planning and investment management firm, and has been guiding families through retirement decisions since 2012. He is an advisor held to the fiduciary standard on the portfolios he manages, and he is insurance licensed, so he can use the right tool for your situation rather than the one tool he happens to sell.

Based in Twin Falls, Idaho, Sean works with families across the country. He wrote this book for the conversations he has every week: clear, direct, and free of the jargon that keeps good people in the dark about their own money.
